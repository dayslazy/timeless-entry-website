Tips & tricks

Keep it rolling

A few minutes of care keeps your door quiet, safe, and out of the repair queue.

Safety first

Never DIY a torsion spring

Springs hold enough tension to cause serious injury. If one snaps, leave the door closed and give us a call.

Monthly check

Test the auto-reverse

Lay a 2×4 flat under the door and close it. It should reverse on contact — if it doesn't, it needs an adjustment.

Twice a year

Lube the moving parts

A garage-door lubricant on hinges, rollers, and springs quiets a noisy door and slows wear.
